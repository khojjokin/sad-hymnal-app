Name: SDA Hymnal
Subtitle: Sing every hymn, every day

Promotional text:
A hymn for every morning, a verse for every evening. Share them as beautiful cards, and lead worship with Projector mode.

Description:
The Seventh day Adventist Hymnal, right on your iPhone.

Find any hymn by number, title or words. Read in large, clear type that stays on screen while you sing. Save the hymns you love and make lists for a service, a choir or your family worship.

Every day brings three hymns of the day, a verse to carry with you, the most sung hymns of the week and the stories behind well loved hymns and their writers. A gentle reminder each morning brings the day's hymn to you.

Share any verse as a card made for Instagram, WhatsApp or your church group.

Your account is yours to choose. Sign in with Apple, Google or email to keep your hymns and lists on every device.

SDA Hymnal Plus
For those who lead and love to share, Plus adds:
Projector mode, to put a hymn on the church screen or a TV one verse at a time
Service planner, to plan each Sabbath and share a printed style programme
Card studio, with more looks, new lettering and your own photo
No ads

Plus is £1.99 a month and renews monthly until you cancel in Settings. Every hymn stays free without it.
