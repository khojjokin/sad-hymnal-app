Name: SDA Hymnal
Subtitle: Sing every hymn, anywhere

Description:
The Seventh day Adventist Hymnal, right on your iPhone.

Find any hymn by number, title or words. Read in large, clear type that stays on screen while you sing. Save the hymns you love, make lists for a service or a choir, and share a verse as a beautiful card.

Each day brings a hymn of the day, songs for the moment and the stories behind well loved hymns and their writers.

An account is optional. Create one to keep your saved hymns and lists on every device.
